Internal Travel Pricing Guide

Flight Pricing

Lagos to Nairobi:
Typical economy fare: USD 450.
Prices are estimates and may vary depending on airline, booking time,
travel date, and availability.

Hotel Pricing

Nairobi:
Typical hotel rate: USD 120 per night.
A standard 3-night stay therefore costs approximately USD 360.

Example Logistics Calculation

A typical Lagos to Nairobi flight plus a 3-night Nairobi hotel stay:
Flight: USD 450
Hotel: USD 360
Estimated total: USD 810.

These figures are internal estimates for planning purposes only and should
not be treated as live booking prices.
